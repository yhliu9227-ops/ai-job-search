### Independent Research — Interactive Acoustic Installation: Urban Noise Mitigation through Origami-Based Helmholtz Resonators (In Progress)
Self-directed research

- Examining how fold pattern, chamber volume, neck geometry, and aperture dimensions affect resonant frequency and sound-absorption performance, drawing on Helmholtz resonance theory and acoustic-metamaterial research

### Degree Project — Stitching Urbanisms: Regenerating Arrival across Shenzhen's Fragmented Urban Villages (08/2025 - 05/2026)
Pratt Institute, School of Architecture — SP26 Degree Project Award and Exhibition
- Investigated Shenzhen's urban villages as informal "arrival cities," focusing on how demolition-led redevelopment displaces the affordable housing and micro-economies that support migrant workers
- Conducted field research in Baishizhou Village, documenting spatial fragmentation, informal economic typologies, and infrastructure gaps through mapping, urbanization-data analysis, and building-typology surveys
- Synthesized urban-studies and critical-geography frameworks, including Doug Saunders's *Arrival City*, David Harvey's *Social Justice and the City*, and Henri Lefebvre's *The Production of Space*, to argue that urban villages are integral, rather than residual, components of the formal city
- Compared informal-settlement regeneration precedents, including Chongqing Shibati Community Renewal and DOFFICE LM Youth Community, to identify transferable strategies for renewal without displacement
- Translated the research into a vertical "arrival center": a mixed-use tower integrating affordable housing, micro-enterprises, and public space
- Evaluated design feasibility through solar, wind, and structural-core studies

### Design Research — Inflatable Structures: Deformation & Prototyping (08/2024 - 12/2024)
Pratt Institute, Design Research Architecture (DRA) Studio
- Formulated and tested hypotheses about how two-dimensional weld patterns (cosine-curve networks, Voronoi packing, directional offsets, triangulated isotropic grids) govern the three-dimensional deformation of pressurized structures
- Designed and fabricated multiple pattern families; ran controlled physical experiments isolating how curve arrangement, cut geometry, and weld-line placement affect shrinkage, stiffness, and surface curvature; extended cell-scale findings to a folded polyhedron, validating predicted fold behavior against inflated outcomes
- Developed Grasshopper simulations of inflation behavior and cross-validated computational outputs against physical prototypes
- Quantified stiffness trade-offs among pattern typologies by applying controlled loads and measuring vertical deformation across iterations
- Applied validated findings to a full-scale, load-bearing inflatable chair; after heat-welded seams failed under stress, redesigned the assembly with sewn seams based on empirical testing
- Compared ripstop nylon, PVC, and TPU for weldability, airtightness, and durability; incorporated recycled materials and investigated end-of-life recycling pathways
- Evaluated the final prototype through user testing focused on comfort, stability, and usability
- Documented the iterative hypothesis-experiment-analysis-application cycle in a design research publication
