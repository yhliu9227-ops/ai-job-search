## Strongest Behaviors
- **Real experimental method, not just iteration:** Isolates single variables (curve arrangement, cut geometry, weld-line placement) through controlled physical experiments, and checks computational predictions against built prototypes rather than trusting simulation alone.
- **Sustains self-directed research without external structure:** The Helmholtz resonator project has no course, client, or deadline behind it - it continues because the candidate is driving it.
- **Field-research grounding:** Went to Baishizhou Village in person to document spatial fragmentation and informal economic typologies rather than working from secondary sources alone, and paired that with theoretical framing (Saunders, Harvey, Lefebvre).
- **Site-to-drawing follow-through:** Takes a project from raw site analysis through 3D modeling, layout drawings, and final presentation materials without dropping detail along the way.
- **Team production reliability:** Delivered consistently across four simultaneous project tracks in one internship, each with a different program type and design phase.

## How You Work Best
- Environments with room for genuine open-ended inquiry, not just execution of a fixed brief - the resonator project shows this is intrinsically motivating, not just tolerated
- Structured studio/production environments with clear phases (analysis, concept, development, presentation) when the work is delivery-mode rather than research-mode
- Team settings with defined roles, where personal ownership of a deliverable (a model, a drawing set, a set of diagrams, a research finding) is clear
- Environments that value both visual/spatial communication (diagrams, renderings, models) and written/analytical communication (theoretical synthesis, documented experimental cycles)
